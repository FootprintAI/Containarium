- **Threat sentry flags outbound DDoS / proxy fan-out** (#3709). A new `OUTBOUND_FANOUT` rule raises a MEDIUM, record-only finding when one container reaches many distinct public addresses in a short window: 15 or more on open-proxy ports (the shape of a DDoS or scraping tool rotating a public proxy list), or 200 or more on non-web ports below 32768 (a scanner). Web, DNS and NTP traffic, replies to clients, private and reserved destinations never count; IPv4 only. Previously no rule saw this traffic, so such a tool ran unnoticed unless a provider firewall happened to match its destination port.
