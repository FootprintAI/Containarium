# Trademark policy

Containarium's source code is licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)). That licence grants broad rights to use, modify and
redistribute the code. It does not grant rights to the project's names and
logos: section 6 of the licence says so explicitly. This policy states what you
may do with the names without asking, and what needs permission.

The aim is simple. You are free to build on, sell support for, host, fork and
write about Containarium. You may not make something that is not the project
look like it is, or like it is endorsed by the project.

## The marks

FootprintAI claims rights in these names and any logos the project publishes
for them:

- **Containarium** (the project, the platform, and the `containarium` and
  `containariumd` programs when used as a product name)
- **Containarium Cloud** (the hosted service)

Other names in this repository, such as the Go module paths, flag names and
file names, are identifiers, not marks.

## What you can do without asking

- **Refer to the project accurately.** "Works with Containarium", "runs on
  Containarium", "a Containarium integration", "compatible with
  Containarium": all fine, in text, in documentation and in headlines, as long
  as it is true and does not suggest the project made or approved your work.
- **Write about it.** Articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons, reviews and
  videos may use the name and show screenshots of the unmodified software.
- **Redistribute unmodified releases.** A mirror, package repository or
  air-gapped copy of the official release files may keep the name, provided it
  serves the files exactly as released. The signatures and checksums on each
  release ([how to verify](docs/security/VERIFYING-RELEASES.md)) are how users
  check that.
- **Package it for a distribution.** Packaging changes that only adapt
  install paths, init integration or dependencies to the platform, and
  security fixes backported from the project, may keep the name. Say what you
  changed.
- **Name a community project after what it works with.** "<your name> for
  Containarium" or "<your name>, a Containarium plugin" is fine when the
  distinguishing part is yours. "Containarium <something>" on its own is not.
- **Resell, host and support it.** You may offer paid support, hosting,
  consulting or a managed service built on Containarium, and may say so
  ("built on Containarium", "Containarium hosting"). Your service needs its own
  name. See the next section for what to avoid.

## What needs written permission

Ask first if you want to:

- use a mark as part of a company, product, service or domain name, or as a
  social media handle or app-store listing name;
- use the project's logo, or a confusingly similar one;
- print the name or logo on merchandise, or use it in advertising that implies
  a partnership;
- describe yourself as an "official", "certified", "authorised" or "approved"
  Containarium partner, reseller or provider;
- distribute a modified version under the name Containarium, beyond the
  packaging changes above.

## What is not allowed

- Calling a fork, or a build with functional changes, "Containarium". Rename
  it, and say it is based on Containarium. You keep every right under the
  Apache licence to make and distribute it.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests the project, FootprintAI or a
  contributor wrote, reviewed, endorses or supports your product or service.
- A name, domain or logo that a reasonable person would mistake for the
  project's own, including misspellings and added words ("Containarium Pro",
  "Containarium Enterprise") for something we do not publish.
- Using the marks to sell or promote anything unrelated, or in a way that is
  misleading, defamatory or disparaging.

## Using the name correctly

Use "Containarium" as a name, capitalised, not as a verb or a plural. The
first time it appears in a page or a document, you may add a line such as:

> Containarium is a trademark of FootprintAI. This is not an official
> Containarium product.

The second sentence is for third-party products only. Do not alter, animate
or recolour the project's logo, and leave clear space around it.

## Contributions

Contributing code or documentation does not give you rights in the marks, and
does not take the project's rights from you. Under section 5 of the Apache
License 2.0, a contribution is licensed under the same terms as the project;
that licence covers the code, not the names.

## Asking for permission, and reporting misuse

To ask for permission, or to report a use that looks like impersonation, open
an issue at <https://github.com/FootprintAI/Containarium/issues> titled
"Trademark: <short description>". Put nothing sensitive in it; a maintainer
will arrange a private channel if one is needed. Say who you are, what you want
to do, and where the name will appear. Reports of misuse should include a link.

We aim to respond, but make no promise about when. A permission is for the use you described
and can be withdrawn if the use changes.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may change. Changes are made in this repository, and the history
of this file is the record. A use that was permitted when it began may be
asked to change if the policy later tightens, with reasonable notice.
